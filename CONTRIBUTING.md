# Team Working Agreement & Contribution Rules

We are setting this up so everyone is on the same page, tasks don't clash, and our final peer evaluations reflect who actually did the work. Please read through this before starting on your tasks.

---

## 1. Ground Rules on Commits & Code

* **No direct pushes to `main` or `dev`.** Always create your own branch first.
* **Keep commits clean and frequent:**
  * Commit small updates as you work rather than uploading a huge zip folder or a 50-file commit the night before a deadline.
  * Write normal, clear commit messages so others know what was changed (e.g., `added attendance checkin form`, not just `updates` or `fix`).
* **Pull Requests (PRs):**
  * When your task is done, open a PR into `dev`.
  * Post the PR link in our WhatsApp/Discord group.
  * At least one other team member needs to test and approve it before merging.

---

## 2. Branch Names

Keep branch names simple:
* Features: `feature/<task-name>` (e.g., `feature/login-screen`, `feature/staff-salary`)
* Bug fixes: `fix/<issue-name>` (e.g., `fix/navbar-overlap`)

---

## 3. Communication & Weekly Syncs

* **Weekly meeting:** We meet once a week (time agreed in the group chat). Attendance is mandatory unless you give a heads-up beforehand.
* **Mid-week update:** Every Wednesday, drop a quick 3-line update in the chat:
  1. What you finished this week.
  2. What you are working on next.
  3. If you are stuck on anything and need help.
* **Ghosting:** If someone disappears or ignores messages for more than 48 hours while holding a critical project task, the task will be reassigned to someone else so the rest of the team isn't blocked.

---

## 4. Task Ownership & Accountability

* Every feature on our task list must have one clear person responsible for it.
* "Group work" does not mean doing nothing and putting your name on the final report.
* Git commit logs, merged PRs, and meeting attendance will be our reference when filling out the university peer evaluation forms at the end of the semester.

---

## 5. Team Acknowledgment

Everyone in the group needs to pull this repository, read these rules, and confirm in the group chat that they agree before starting on sprint tasks.
